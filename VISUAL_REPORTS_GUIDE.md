## 🎯 What Each Section Shows

### 💰 SALES SECTION
**Purpose:** Quick overview of current sales performance

Shows:
- 📈 Total sales number
- 💵 Total money made
- 🏆 Best selling products
- 📋 Latest transactions
- 📊 Category breakdown

**Good for:** Quick daily check of sales status

### 📊 REPORTS SECTION
**Purpose:** Detailed analysis and record keeping

Shows:
- 📊 Complete statistics
- 📁 Category analysis
- 📦 Product history (every product, every sale)
- 🧾 Bills organized by date
- 📋 Complete transaction log

**Good for:** 
- Understanding trends
- Finding best-selling products
- Tracking sales history
- Generating reports
- Finding when specific items were sold

---

## 📅 Date & Time Information

**Every sale includes:**
```
Date: YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2026-03-18)
Time: HH:MM:SS (e.g., 14:30:45)
```

**Benefits:**
- Know exactly when each product was sold
- See sales patterns by time
- Generate daily/weekly/monthly reports
- Track inventory changes over time

## 💡 Using the Reports

### To Find:

**"How much did Product X sell for?"**
→ Go to Reports → Product-Wise Sales History → Find product → See total revenue

**"What sold on a specific date?"**
→ Go to Reports → Bill Reports by Date → Find that date → See all products

**"Which product sold the most?"**
→ Go to Sales → Top Selling Products → See ranked list

**"How much money did we make today?"**
→ Go to Sales → Quick Overview → See Total Revenue

**"What was the complete list of all sales?"**
→ Go to Reports → Complete Transaction Log → See everything

## 📊 Data Points Tracked

For each sale, the system tracks:
```
✅ Product Name
✅ Category (Beauty, Electronics, etc)
✅ Unit Price
✅ Quantity Sold
✅ Total Amount (Price × Quantity)
✅ Date (YYYY-MM-DD)
✅ Time (HH:MM:SS)
✅ Exact Timestamp
```
